DepGuard Privacy Policy

Offline dependency analysis for Android.

Effective 6 October 2026 · Last updated 6 October 2026

No data collected · No internet permission

1. Summary

DepGuard does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no server component. It declares zero Android permissions — including no INTERNET permission — so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

Everything you paste is analysed on your device, and the results are stored only on your device.

3. Data the app processes

When you paste build output, the app reads it as plain text and parses it locally. This can include:

  • Dependency coordinates, for example androidx.core:core-ktx:1.15.0.
  • A project label you type yourself, such as a module or project name.
  • Lines from Gradle output, libs.versions.toml or build scripts that you choose to paste.

This text is processed in memory during analysis. The raw text you pasted is not retained — only the normalised result described below is written to local storage.

4. What is stored on your device

Stored locally, in an on-device database:

  • A history entry: an identifier, creation time, your project label, dependency count, issue count, health score, detected input format and a toolchain snapshot.
  • The parsed dependencies: group, artifact, version, scope, origin and the source line they came from.
  • The findings: type, severity, subject, details and location.

Stored locally, in on-device preferences: appearance mode, whether informational and heuristic findings are shown, dynamic colour, and whether onboarding has been completed.

Nothing in this list is ever transmitted off the device.

6. Clipboard

The Paste and Copy report actions use the Android system clipboard, and only when you tap those buttons. The app does not read the clipboard in the background, does not watch it for changes, and does not send clipboard contents anywhere.

7. Permissions

DepGuard requests no Android permissions at runtime and declares none in its manifest. There is no storage permission, no camera, no microphone, no location and no internet access.

8. Backup and device transfer

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are explicitly disabled for this app's database and preferences, so analysis history is not copied to Google's servers or to another device.

9. Third parties

No dependency-analysis data is shared with third parties, because nothing is transmitted at all. The app contains no analytics, attribution, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. Google Play itself handles app installation and store interactions under Google's own policies.

10. Children

DepGuard is a developer tool and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information of any kind, it does not knowingly collect data from children of any age.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Analysis history stays on your device until you delete it.
  • You can delete individual results from the History screen.
  • Settings → Clear all local data removes every stored analysis and resets all preferences.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data.

12. Security

Because your data never leaves the device, there is no server-side breach surface, no data transit and no third-party storage to secure. Data at rest is held in the app's private storage, which Android isolates per application.

13. Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date. If DepGuard ever gains a feature that requires a permission or network access, this policy will be revised before that feature ships, and the change will be stated plainly.
